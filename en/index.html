Prepare for Spain's driving theory test

Questions explained with the law or the DGT publication behind them, practice tests like the real exam and the 2026 RGC reform. No ads, no account.

  • Every answer, with its source. Open the BOE article or the DGT publication, with the words that decide the answer highlighted. It works offline.
  • Practice tests like the exam. 30 questions, 30 minutes and no more than 3 mistakes. You can change your answers before you submit.
  • Your mistakes come back until you get them right on different days.
  • In English and Spanish. In practice sessions, one tap shows any question in the other language.
  • Your theory test without a driving school: the DGT process, step by step.

Help

Restore the Pase. In the app, go to Settings › Pase › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel the subscription. On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Leyvia.

Found a mistake in a question? Email halilgoktepe.dev@gmail.com with the question. We check it against the official source.
