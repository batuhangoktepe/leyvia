2. What the App is

Leyvia helps you prepare for the theory test for the class B driver's license in Spain: questions with explanations, practice tests, mistake review and a step-by-step guide to the DGT process. Every answer shows its source: an article of Spanish law published in the BOE (Spain's official gazette) or a DGT publication.

Leyvia is an independent app. It is not affiliated with the DGT or any public authority, and its questions are not the official exam questions. We review the content carefully, but it may contain errors and the rules can change: if in doubt, check the official sources. Using the App does not guarantee that you will pass the exam.
