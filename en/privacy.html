Privacy

Leyvia is built to keep your studying on your iPhone.

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone. Your answers, practice tests, mistakes, your exam date, the result you enter for your real exam and your settings are stored only on your device and in its backups, if you have them turned on. We have no access to them. Deleting the app deletes them from your iPhone.
  • No ads, no tracking. Leyvia shows no ads, uses no analytics and never tracks you across other apps or websites. It includes no third-party tools that collect data.
  • Purchases. If you buy the Pase, Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details. To know whether the Pase is active, the app asks Apple from your iPhone.
  • "Ask the law." On compatible iPhones, this feature uses Apple Intelligence on the device itself: your questions never leave your iPhone.
  • Listen. Read-aloud uses your iPhone's voices, also on the device.
  • Links. Some sources open official websites (DGT, BOE) in your browser. Those websites have their own policies.
  • Ratings. After you pass your first practice test or your real exam, the app may ask Apple to show its rating prompt; Apple handles your answer.
  • Email. If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply to you and to fix what you report.

If a future version changes any of this, this page and the App Store privacy label will say exactly what is collected and why, before that version ships.
